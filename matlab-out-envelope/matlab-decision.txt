Interpretation rules:
- PowerEnvelopeCandidate means >=5 included runs were all safe and all
  moved >0.1 deg, matching the docs/control-a2-alignment-hardware-test.md
  "5/5 run" gate. Fewer than 5 runs can never set this true.
- PowerEnvelopeCandidate is a necessary, not sufficient, condition for the
  official gate: it does not check PrimeStateValid/EnableStateValid/
  EnablePowerPpm or zero-fault health counters. Confirm those from the
  summary CSV directly before treating a profile as gate-passed.
- WithinOneDegCluster is diagnostic only, not an official acceptance gate.
- MATLAB never authorizes a higher hardware power after a hard-gate fault.
- The power-ceiling estimate is a straight-line fit through a handful of
  power levels, not a physical model; a level that already faulted (see
  FaultKinds above) is the real ceiling evidence, not the extrapolation.
