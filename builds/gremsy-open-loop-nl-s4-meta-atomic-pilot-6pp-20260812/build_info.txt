Purpose
-------
Replacement S4 hardware pilot for the 6-pole-pair Gremsy-compatible pure
open-loop NL path. It supersedes the earlier S0-S3 pilot artifact after the
first JIG8 log proved its 1900-byte META buffer truncated RunRole and
EligibleForStatistics and joined the following GRID record to META.

Correction
----------
- LogLineLarge buffer: 1900 -> 2600 bytes for all profiles.
- Large structured records are atomic: overflow emits
  LOG_ERROR,Reason=LARGE_LINE_TRUNCATED, increments the UART/logging failure
  counter and never transmits a plausible truncated prefix.
- Compiler stack report: LogLineLarge=2616 bytes,
  PrintSweepLog.constprop=4048 bytes, within the dedicated 12288-byte task.
- Measurement/control algorithm is unchanged from the S0-S3 pilot.

Required first-log check
------------------------
- META is one complete line ending with all batch fields including
  RunRole and EligibleForStatistics.
- GRID starts on its own line.
- PRECONDITION has EligibleForStatistics=0.
- Ten OFFICIAL runs have EligibleForStatistics=1.
- No LOG_ERROR and END.UartTransmitFailures=0.
